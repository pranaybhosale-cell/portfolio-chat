PROJECT / 001 In progress
DATA ENGINEERING · RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS

Maharashtra Livestock Diversity Dataset

A district-level livestock dataset designed around responsible data handling: consistent labels, source traceability and honest treatment of missing information.

THE CHALLENGE

Official livestock statistics differ in definition, reference year and source type. Combining them carelessly risks misleading comparisons.

MY APPROACH

Standardise district names and indicators, keep source metadata, and separate census counts from annual estimates or modelled values.
